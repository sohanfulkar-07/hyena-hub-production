Privacy Policy & IP Rights

Last updated: July 2026

1. Data Protection & SSL Encryption

At The Hyena Hub, we prioritize the confidentiality of creative submissions, screenplays, and executive screening interactions. All submissions transmitted through our Creator Intake Portal undergo high-grade end-to-end SSL encryption.

2. Private Theater Forensic DRM

Screenings delivered via our Private Theater feature dynamic forensic watermarking keyed to the authenticated viewer's IP address, timestamp, and executive identifier to prevent unauthorized capture or distribution.

3. Intellectual Property Integrity

Submitting a pitch deck or screenplay via our portal does not transfer intellectual property rights to The Hyena Hub. Creators retain ownership of submitted original materials until a formal optioning or co-production contract is executed.
